Even Terminal OpenCode

AI sessions on Even G2 glasses

Node.js OpenCode

Connects Even Terminal directly to OpenCode so I can follow AI sessions, approve actions, and answer agents’ questions from Even G2 glasses. Shows live responses and compact summaries of what the agent is doing.

Keeps session history available while agents work and restores pending questions and approvals after a disconnect. Each approval stays tied to its session. A missing or invalid answer never grants permission.
